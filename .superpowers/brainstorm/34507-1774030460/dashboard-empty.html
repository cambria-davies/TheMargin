Design Notes

ZEROES, NOT DASHES

Stats show "0" rather than "—" or being hidden. The structure is all there, just waiting to be filled. The user sees exactly what will populate.

EVOCATIVE + ACTIONABLE

"The first page is the hardest. Everything after is momentum." replaces the streak counter. Below: "Tap the pen to log your first session." — the nudge.

TODAY DOT GLOWS

The 4th ink dot (today) has an amber border + glow even when empty — it's asking to be filled. The other 6 are neutral outlines.

SINGLE PAGE STACK

The manuscript stack is one blank page with a curled corner — already spec'd. Inviting, not sad. It's the same component, just with pageCount = 0.

After First Session

Once the user logs their first session, the evocative message is replaced by the standard streak counter ("1 day streak"), the today dot fills amber, stats count up, and the save-to-stack animation adds their first pages. The empty state resolves itself naturally — no special dismissal logic needed.
