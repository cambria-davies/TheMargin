Tier Breakdown

Tier 1: 0 sessions

Fully empty. Ghost chart silhouette at 8% opacity — just enough to hint at what's coming. A writing quote (from the bundled tips) + "Log your first session and your patterns will start to take shape." Centered, generous spacing.

Tier 2: 1–6 sessions

Unlocked: Writing calendar (works with any data), stat cards (avg words, avg duration, best day, this week), streaks, mood distribution, goal progress.
Locked: Words by day of week and weekly trend show ghost chart silhouettes with "N more sessions to unlock." These need 7+ data points to be statistically meaningful.

Tier 3: 7+ sessions

Everything unlocked. Charts animate in on first reveal. No locked sections, no hints — just data. This is the steady state.

Why These Tiers?

Calendar, stat cards, streaks, and mood work with even 1 data point — no reason to gate them. But "best day of the week" with 3 sessions across 2 days isn't insight, it's noise. The bar chart and trend line need a week of data to mean anything. The ghost silhouettes with countdown create a progression reward: every session gets you closer to unlocking the full picture.
