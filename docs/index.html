Support

hello.themargin.app@gmail.com

  • Is The Margin free?

    The Margin is free to download with no ads and no subscriptions.

  • Where is my data stored?

    All your data stays on your device. The Margin doesn't collect analytics, require an account, or send data to any server.

  • Can I export my sessions?

    Yes. You can export all your writing sessions as a CSV file from the Settings screen.

  • What devices are supported?

    The Margin requires iOS 17 or later. It works on all iPhones that support iOS 17.

  • I found a bug or have a feature request.

    Send us an email at hello.themargin.app@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.
